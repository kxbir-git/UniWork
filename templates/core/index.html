How UniWork Operates

Built from the ground up for safe university collaboration

01
Post or Bid

Clients post academic or commercial tasks with clear criteria. Freelancers submit targeted proposals matching their expertise.

02
Milestone Escrow

03

Freelancers submit deliverables and documentation. Clients inspect, approve, and instantly release milestone earnings.
