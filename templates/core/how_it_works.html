How UniWork Operates

A clear, transparent lifecycle for both Clients and Freelancers

For Clients

  1. Post a Detailed Job
    Specify deliverables, required university skills, deadline, and fixed or hourly budget.
  2. Review Proposals & Shortlist
    Inspect candidate profiles, GitHub/portfolio links, hourly rates, and personalized pitches.
  3. Accept Proposal & Escrow Locking
    Accepting automatically activates the Contract room and locks milestone funds in simulated escrow.
  4. Review Deliverables & Approve
    Inspect work attachments, discuss iterations via threaded chat, and click "Approve" to release funds.
Post a Job Now

For Student Freelancers

  1. Create Profile & Highlight Skills
    List relevant degree coursework, project repositories, portfolio link, and desired hourly rate.
  2. Browse & Submit Targeted Proposals
    Search projects matching your domain and submit bids with estimated delivery timeframes.
  3. Collaborate in the Contract Room
    Chat with client, align on milestone dates, and track deliverable status with real-time feedback.
  4. Submit Work & Receive Earnings
    Upload work notes and code attachments. On client approval, funds are immediately added to your wallet.
