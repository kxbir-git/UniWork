Academic Context & Design

About UniWork

UniWork is a complete, full-stack freelancing web platform developed as an undergraduate final year university computer science project.

Problem Statement & Motivation

Undergraduate and postgraduate students often possess strong software engineering, AI/ML, design, and academic research skills, yet traditional freelancing platforms (e.g. Upwork, Fiverr) present high barriers to entry, steep platform commissions, and impersonal bidding environments. UniWork solves this by establishing a dedicated campus-oriented marketplace with transparent simulated escrow tracking.

Architecture & Engineering Highlights

  • Custom Authentication System: Extended AbstractUser with email-based authentication and role enforcement (is_client, is_freelancer).
  • Simulated Escrow Ledger: Wallet and Transaction models providing double-entry accounting mechanics for milestone locking, release, and auditability.
  • Role-Based Permission Mixins: Strict access control separating client privileges (job creation, proposal acceptance, milestone release) from freelancer privileges (bidding, deliverable uploads).
  • Contract Workroom: Synchronized milestone timeline, attachment upload handling, and embedded direct messaging.
